# Dragon Emperor IV: The Eternal War

Playable demo of Taiyo's fictional 1997 strategy RPG for the Taiyo Vision 32-bit system.

A miniature war-table kingdom drops into a stereoscopic 100-vs-100 sprite battle. View it in mono, red/cyan, side-by-side or cross-eye stereo on a desktop, or in WebXR on a Meta Quest (open the GitHub Pages link in the Quest Browser and press Enter VR).

## Controls
- Desktop: drag to orbit, right-drag or WASD to pan, Q/E to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom down to ground level. 1/2/3 set Charge/Hold/Spread. Space or the Starfall button enters aim mode; click the field to drop the meteors, Esc cancels.
- Quest: grip to grab and move the table; both grips to scale and turn it, from war table to standing among the soldiers. Left stick slides the battlefield, right stick turns and zooms, B resets. Point the right controller like a gun to aim Starfall at the rune reticle; right trigger fires it when charged (otherwise it orders a charge), left trigger cycles orders.

Dragon Emperor I not required.
