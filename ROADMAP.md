# Dragon Emperor IV roadmap

## Done
- Skirmish mode, 12 relics, five new troop types and six generals, castle sieges, weekly castle tasks with loyalty and wandering generals, the Ashen Wyrm boss and Ysolde's Eclipse, New Game+ Ages
- Stereoscopic 400 vs 400 sprite battles with duels, morale, orders, capture and persuasion
- Seven battlefield biomes with obstacles and flow-field pathfinding
- Campaign map: 18 castles, roads, real-time armies, three nations (Aldmere, Ashen Legion, Duskmarch)
- Generals who level up, gain rank and command more troops

## Next
1. **Castle sieges.** Defenders behind walls with a gate the attackers must break; castles as places to rest and resupply.
2. **Weekly cycle and castle management.** Rest, search for wandering generals and items, fortify walls, reward generals; loyalty and defection.
3. **More troop types.** Monks (heal), mages (back-line spells), harpies or wyverns (fly over rivers, walls and cliffs), beasts, heavy knights, dragonriders.
4. **Pre-battle formations and deployment.** Line, wedge, spread, square, ambush; choose where to deploy.
5. **Nations, rulers and story.** Pick a ruler; story scenes between weeks with talking portraits over the war table.
6. **Items and relics.** Swords, armor and charms that boost duel stats, troop limits or spell power.
7. **Multi-general battles.** Several generals on the field per side, each with his own troops and spell; select one general (or all) to give orders.
